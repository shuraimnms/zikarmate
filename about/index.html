🌙 About DeenPlus

📱 What is DeenPlus?

DeenPlus is a powerful Islamic app designed to help Muslims learn, recite, and memorize daily duas and azkar.

✨ Features

  • ✅ Duas & Azkar: Includes Hisnul Muslim, Morning & Evening Azkar
  • ✅ Quran Recitation: Listen to beautiful recitations
  • ✅ Themes: Customize the app's look and feel
  • ✅ Offline Mode: Use the app even without internet
  • ✅ Favorites & Folders: Save important duas for quick access
  • ✅ Simple UI: Clean and easy-to-use design

📖 How to Use?

  • 🟢 Browse: Navigate through different categories of duas
  • 🟢 Listen: Play audio recitations for better learning
  • 🟢 Bookmark: Save your favorite duas for quick access
  • 🟢 Customize: Switch themes and font sizes for comfort

⚠️ User Guidelines

  • 📌 Authentic Islamic Source: All duas are referenced from reliable books
  • 📌 Respect & Usage: The app is for Islamic guidance, avoid misuse
  • 📌 Continuous Updates: More features will be added based on user feedback
  • 📌 Feedback: Share your thoughts and suggestions for improvements

  • 📌 Privacy & Security: We follow best practices for data protection
